Cement Guide

Types of Cement

Understand the technical characteristics and optimal applications for each cement category available at Uttam Traders.

OPC

OPC Cement (Ordinary Portland Cement)

High-strength cement commonly used for structural work, RCC, columns, beams, heavy load structures, and pre-cast concrete.

Best Use: Columns, Beams, RCC Slabs, Bridges
Benefits: Rapid early strength, fast formwork removal
PPC

PPC Cement (Portland Pozzolana Cement)

Durable cement with good workability, commonly used for residential construction, brickwork mortar, and general plastering.

Best Use: Home Building, Plastering, Brickwork
Benefits: Anti-crack properties, moisture seepage protection
PSC

PSC Cement (Portland Slag Cement)

Offers exceptional durability and is suitable for aggressive coastal environments, soil dampness, and sewage tanks.

Best Use: Coastal Structures, Marine Works, Water Tanks
Benefits: High sulphate & chloride corrosion resistance
RHC

Rapid Hardening Cement

Develops strength significantly faster than ordinary cement. Essential where early structural load-bearing capacity is required.

Best Use: Highway Repairs, Pre-cast Concrete, Emergency Repairs
Benefits: 3-day strength equals 7-day OPC strength
SRC

Sulphate Resistant Cement

Designed specifically for subterranean applications and soils containing high concentrations of sulphate salts.

Best Use: Subsoil Foundations, Chemical Plants, Piles
Benefits: Prevents concrete expansion and crumbling
WHITE

White Cement

Manufactured with iron-free raw materials. Used primarily for architectural finishes, terrazzo flooring, wall putty, and tile grouting.

Best Use: Tile Grouting, Architectural Moulding, Wall Primer
Benefits: Pure white aesthetic, smooth adhesive bond
MASONRY

Masonry Cement

Specially formulated blend with enhanced plasticity and air entrainment for smooth brick laying and exterior wall plastering.

Best Use: Block Work, Bricklaying, Rendering
Benefits: Superior trowel workability, lower shrinkage

Steel Engineering

TMT Bar Grades

Understanding TMT yield strength and ductility levels to specify the right grade for your structural engineer's drawings.

Fe 415
Yield Strength: 415 N/mm²

Suitable for smaller residential construction, stirrups, and light load non-structural framing work.

Fe 500
Yield Strength: 500 N/mm²

Standard benchmark grade used widely for residential house construction, commercial framing, and slabs.

Fe 500D
Higher Ductility Grade

Features higher elongation capability (16%). Top choice for earthquake zones and critical RCC columns.

Fe 550
Yield Strength: 550 N/mm²

High strength structural grade specified for industrial plants, bridges, and heavy load underground works.

Fe 550D
Super Ductile Heavy Grade

Combines maximum 550 yield strength with high strain hardening for high-rise towers and heavy bridges.
